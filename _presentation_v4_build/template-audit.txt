Communication job
  Turn the existing aerospace controls and DO-178C training deck into a practical first-session guide without weakening its assurance message. A first-time user should know which model to open, how update/run/build differ, where to find the relevant Simulink toolstrip controls, how to review units and signal metadata, why the Simulation Data Inspector is useful, and how an illustrative requirement traces to executed evidence.
